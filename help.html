🛡️ Safety Tips for Helpers

📍
Always suggest a public spot
Cafes, lobbies, plazas — somewhere well-lit and busy. Never a private location.
⏱️
Respond quickly
People requesting charges are often in urgent situations. Fast responses earn better ratings.
⭐
Rate every interaction
Your ratings help keep the community trustworthy. Always leave an honest review after helping.
❌
Cancel anytime — no explanation needed
If anything feels off about a request, cancel immediately. Your safety always comes first.
